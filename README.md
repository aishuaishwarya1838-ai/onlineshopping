# Online Shopping System – Project Explanation

An Online Shopping System is a web-based application that allows customers to purchase products through the internet without visiting a physical store. Customers can browse products, search for items, add products to a shopping cart, place orders, make payments, and track their deliveries.

## 1. Project title

ONLINE SHOPPING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## 2. Introduction

The Online Shopping System is designed to make shopping easier, faster, and more convenient for customers.

In traditional shopping, customers need to visit shops, search for products, and wait in queues to purchase items. This system solves these problems by providing an online platform where customers can buy products from anywhere and at any time.

The application provides two main users:

- Admin: Manages products, categories, customers, orders, and payments.
- Customer: Registers, logs in, searches for products, adds items to the cart, and purchases products.

## 3. Objectives of the project

The main objectives are:

1. To provide an easy online shopping platform.
2. To save customers' time and effort.
3. To display different products with prices and descriptions.
4. To allow customers to purchase products online.
5. To provide secure login and registration.
6. To manage customer orders efficiently.
7. To reduce manual work in shopping management.
8. To provide order tracking and payment information.

## 6. System workflow

The working process is:

1. Customer opens the website.
2. Customer registers or logs in.
3. Customer views available products.
4. Customer searches for required products.
5. Customer adds products to the shopping cart.
6. Customer proceeds to checkout.
7. Customer enters delivery details.
8. Customer selects a payment method.
9. Order is successfully placed.
10. Admin receives and processes the order.
11. Customer tracks the order until delivery.
